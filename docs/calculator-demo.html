Tuning it

Every assumption is a named constant at the top of tank-calculator.js: RAIN_IN (monthly rainfall), COLLECTION_EFFICIENCY, IRRIGATION_IN, POOL_IN, GPD, TANKS and RESERVE_DAYS. Change a number there and the whole tool follows.

What drives the recommendation

RESERVE_DAYS is the one to look at first. It is set to 90 — the tank must hold ninety days of peak-season use with no rain at all — and it governs most results. A monthly balance run on average rainfall gives every month some rain, which sizes a family home at under a thousand gallons; averages smooth away the drought a tank exists to cover. The balance is still checked, and takes over when demand outruns supply seasonally.

Embedding

Paste webflow-embed-calculator.txt into a Webflow Embed element. data-cta adds the contact button; data-heading="off" drops the built-in heading when the section supplies its own.
